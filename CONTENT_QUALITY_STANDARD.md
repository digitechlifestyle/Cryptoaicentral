# Crypto AI Central Content Quality Standard

This file defines the minimum publishing standard for Crypto AI Central pages, guides and cards.

## Article Minimum Standard

Every evergreen guide should include:

- Minimum target length: 1,500+ words.
- Clear H1 title.
- H2/H3 heading structure.
- Strong introduction with TL;DR.
- Practical examples.
- Risk warnings.
- Best-practice checklist.
- Comparison table where useful.
- FAQ section.
- Glossary where useful.
- Conclusion with next steps.
- At least one relevant image.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- Image caption.
- Internal links to related guides.
- External source links or citations where factual support is needed.
- No guaranteed-profit language.

## Card Minimum Standard

Every guide/listing card should include:

- Visible image or visual header.
- Category label.
- Strong title.
- Useful excerpt.
- Reading time or word count signal.
- Link to the matching full page.
- Clear hover/click state.

## SEO Minimum Standard

Every important content page should include:

- Unique title.
- Meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph title, description and image.
- Twitter card image.
- Crawlable internal links.
- Sitemap entry.

## Monetisation Standard

Money pages and affiliate sections should:

- Help the reader first.
- Include clear affiliate/disclosure language where needed.
- Compare tools fairly.
- Explain who each tool is for and who should avoid it.
- Avoid pressure tactics.
- Avoid guaranteed earnings claims.

## Current Priority

1. Keep original site design.
2. Continue adding long-form guide content.
3. Ensure Blog, Learn and guide cards display images and useful excerpts.
4. Ensure each card links to the correct destination.
5. Keep sitemaps updated as new guides are added.
