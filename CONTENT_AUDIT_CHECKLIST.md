# Crypto AI Central Content Audit Checklist

This checklist defines the minimum quality standard for every public page, guide, blog card and learning section on Crypto AI Central.

## 1. Article Quality Standard

Each guide article must include:

- Minimum target length: 1,500+ words.
- Clear H1 title.
- Logical H2/H3 structure.
- Intro with clear reader benefit.
- TL;DR summary.
- Practical examples.
- Risk warnings.
- Best-practice checklist.
- Tools/resources section.
- Comparison table where useful.
- FAQ section.
- Glossary where useful.
- Clear conclusion and next step.

## 2. Image Standard

Each article must include:

- At least one relevant image or visual block.
- Descriptive alt text.
- Caption explaining what the image shows.
- Open Graph/social image metadata where possible.
- No broken image URLs.

## 3. Card Standard

Each blog, learn or category card must include:

- Relevant title.
- Category badge.
- Helpful excerpt.
- Reading time or word-count indicator.
- Image/visual header where possible.
- Link to the exact page the card represents.
- No dead cards.
- No placeholder-only cards.

## 4. Navigation and Tab Standard

Each major navigation area must lead to useful content:

- Home: useful introduction, categories, featured content and monetisation blocks.
- Blog: full article cards and working search/filter.
- Learn: learning paths and guide links.
- Browse: tool directory or fallback educational content.
- Featured: featured tools or fallback explainers.
- Compare: useful comparison content, not empty tables.
- About: trust, mission and editorial standards.
- Advertise: sponsor/affiliate information.
- Contact: clear contact route.

## 5. SEO Standard

Each public page should include:

- Unique page title.
- Meta description.
- Crawlable internal links.
- Useful headings.
- Sitemap coverage.
- Canonical URL where possible.
- No thin content.
- No duplicate low-value pages.

## 6. Monetisation Standard

Revenue sections must support the reader, not replace content:

- Ad placements should not dominate the page.
- Affiliate/comparison sections must be useful.
- Sponsored content must be clear.
- No guaranteed-profit language.
- Risk warnings must remain visible.

3. Confirm Blog cards display images and link to GuidePost pages.
